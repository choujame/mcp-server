# /commercial-legal:brief

Produce a concise legal briefing on the topic, document, or matter provided.

## Instructions

1. Identify the legal question or issue to be briefed.
2. Produce a structured briefing covering:
   - **Issue**: What is the precise legal question?
   - **Applicable law / framework**: Governing statute, regulation, or common law principle
   - **Key facts**: Relevant factual context
   - **Analysis**: Application of law to facts
   - **Options / Recommendations**: 2–4 options with pros/cons
   - **Recommended action**: The preferred course with rationale
3. Use plain language — assume the reader is a senior business executive, not a lawyer.
4. Keep to 500 words or fewer unless the complexity warrants more.

If the user provides a document, summarise it and brief on the key legal implications.

## Output Format

```
LEGAL BRIEFING
Issue: [One sentence]
Prepared: [Date]

BACKGROUND
[2–3 sentences of context]

ANALYSIS
[Law applied to facts — plain language]

OPTIONS
1. [Option] — Pros: [...] | Cons: [...]
2. [Option] — Pros: [...] | Cons: [...]

RECOMMENDATION
[Clear recommended action with rationale]

NEXT STEPS
[Numbered list of immediate actions]
```

> **Attorney review required.** This briefing is an AI-generated draft and does not constitute legal advice.
