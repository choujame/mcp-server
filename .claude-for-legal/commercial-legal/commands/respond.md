# /commercial-legal:respond

Draft a legal response letter or email based on the context provided.

## Instructions

1. Identify the type of response needed:
   - Response to a contract dispute / breach notice
   - Response to a regulatory enquiry or complaint
   - Response to a legal demand or cease-and-desist
   - Response to a counterparty's redlines or negotiation position
   - General legal correspondence
2. Produce a professional draft response that:
   - Acknowledges receipt (if responding to an inbound communication)
   - States the client's position clearly and concisely
   - Preserves legal rights (without prejudice / without admission)
   - Requests any information or action needed from the other side
   - Sets a clear deadline for response or next steps
3. Tone: professional and firm, but not aggressive unless the situation warrants it.
4. Include a "Without Prejudice" header where appropriate for settlement discussions.

If context is insufficient to draft a response, ask clarifying questions before drafting.

## Output Format

```
[Letterhead placeholder]
[Date]

[Addressee name and address]

Re: [Subject line]

Dear [Name / Counsel],

[Opening — context and purpose]

[Body — client's position, any admissions or denials, legal rights reserved]

[Closing — next steps, deadline, contact information]

Yours sincerely / faithfully,

[Signature block]
```

> **Attorney review required.** This draft must be reviewed and approved by qualified legal counsel before sending.
