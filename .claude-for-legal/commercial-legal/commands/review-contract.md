# /commercial-legal:review-contract

Perform a full structured review of the contract or agreement provided.

## Instructions

1. Read the entire document before beginning the review.
2. Apply the contract-review skill framework.
3. Produce the structured output: overview → key commercial terms → risk flags (High/Medium/Low) → missing provisions → recommended redlines.
4. For each risk flag, cite the specific clause or section number.
5. For each recommended redline, provide the current language verbatim and the suggested replacement.

If no document is provided, ask the user to paste the contract text or share the file path.

## Output

Deliver the full contract review report in the structured format defined by the contract-review skill. End with:

> **Attorney review required.** This analysis is a draft prepared by AI and does not constitute legal advice. All redlines and risk assessments should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before relying on them.
