# DSAR Response Skill

Guide and draft responses to data subject rights requests under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and equivalent frameworks.

## DSAR Handling Workflow

### Step 1: Request Intake & Triage
Assess the request:
- **Request type**: Access (SAR) / Erasure / Portability / Rectification / Restriction / Objection / Opt-out (CCPA)
- **Applicable law**: GDPR (EU/UK) / CCPA (California) / Other
- **Deadline**: GDPR = 1 month (extendable to 3 months for complex requests); CCPA = 45 days (extendable to 90 days)
- **Identity verified?** — Do not respond until identity is confirmed.

### Step 2: Identity Verification
Recommend appropriate verification steps:
- Not overly burdensome (ICO guidance)
- Proportionate to sensitivity of data at stake
- Do not ask for more information than necessary

### Step 3: Exemption Analysis
Check whether any exemptions apply:

**GDPR Exemptions (Art. 23 / Recital 73):**
- Legal privilege
- Prevention of crime
- Tax / public finance
- Regulatory functions
- Legal proceedings
- Health & social work (disproportionate effort)
- Research / statistics / archiving

**CCPA Exemptions (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.105):**
- Legal obligation to retain
- Completing the transaction
- Security / fraud prevention
- Free speech / another's legal rights
- Research (public interest)

### Step 4: Data Search Scope
Define systems to search:
- CRM / customer database
- Email systems
- Marketing platforms
- Analytics / tracking
- HR systems (if employee)
- Backup systems
- Third-party processors

### Step 5: Response Drafting
Produce the response letter with:
- Confirmation of request receipt date and deadline
- Identity verification result
- Scope of personal data found (or confirmation of no data held)
- Any exemptions applied (with explanation)
- Data provided or deletion confirmed
- Right to complain to supervisory authority (GDPR)

## Response Letter Template

```
[Date]
[Data Subject Name & Address]

Re: Response to your [Data Subject Access / Erasure / Portability] Request

Dear [Name],

We confirm receipt of your request dated [date] under [GDPR Article 15 / CCPA § 1798.100 / other].

[IDENTITY VERIFICATION: confirmed by [method] on [date]]

[SCOPE OF DATA FOUND / NOT FOUND]

[EXEMPTIONS APPLIED (if any): We are unable to provide [X] because [exemption and reason]]

[DATA PROVIDED: Please find enclosed / available for download at [link]]

[ERASURE CONFIRMATION: We have deleted / anonymised your personal data from [systems] on [date]. Note: [any retained data and legal basis]]

You have the right to lodge a complaint with [ICO / relevant DPA / California AG].

[SIGNATURE BLOCK]
```

## Guardrails

- Never disclose third-party personal data in a DSAR response.
- Legal privilege review must be conducted before disclosure of any legal advice.
- All draft responses require attorney / DPO sign-off before sending.
- Document the full decision trail for each exemption applied.
- Deadlines are firm — escalate immediately if the deadline is at risk.
