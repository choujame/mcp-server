# /privacy-legal:privacy-review

Conduct a privacy review of the product feature, process, or contract clause provided.

## Instructions

1. Apply the privacy-analysis skill framework.
2. Identify applicable privacy frameworks (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) based on jurisdiction/market.
3. Map data flows: collection → storage → processing → sharing → deletion.
4. Assess legal basis for each processing activity.
5. Identify risk areas and determine whether a DPIA is triggered.
6. List compliance gaps with specific regulatory citations.
7. Provide prioritised remediation recommendations.

If insufficient context is provided, ask for: (a) description of the feature/process, (b) types of personal data involved, (c) applicable jurisdictions.

## Output

Deliver the full privacy analysis in the structured format defined by the privacy-analysis skill. End with:

> **DPO/Attorney review required.** This analysis is a draft prepared by AI. All conclusions regarding lawfulness of processing must be confirmed by qualified privacy counsel or your DPO before relying on them.
