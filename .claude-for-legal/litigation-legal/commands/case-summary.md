# /litigation-legal:case-summary

Produce a structured case summary for the matter described or documents provided.

## Instructions

1. Apply the case-analysis skill framework.
2. Summarise: parties, jurisdiction, procedural stage, key facts (disputed vs. undisputed).
3. Map legal issues to elements and assess strength of each.
4. Produce an evidence map identifying gaps requiring investigation.
5. Assess damages / remedy range.
6. Provide a strategic assessment with recommended next steps.

If documents or case details are not provided, ask the user to describe the matter or paste relevant excerpts.

## Output

Deliver the full case analysis in the structured format defined by the case-analysis skill. End with:

> **Attorney review required.** This summary is an AI-generated draft prepared for legal analysis purposes. It is protected by attorney-client privilege / legal professional privilege. Do not share outside the legal team without counsel's approval.
